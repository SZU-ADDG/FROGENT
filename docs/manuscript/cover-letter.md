# Cover letter

Dear Editors of *Communications Biology*,

We submit the revised manuscript, “FROGENT: An End-to-End Full-process Drug Design Multi-Agent System” (`COMMSBIO-26-4686-T`), for further consideration.

Computational drug discovery now offers capable models for individual steps, yet converting those components into an evidence-controlled scientific workflow remains difficult. FROGENT addresses this gap through a multi-agent framework that coordinates retrieval, molecular and peptide design, structural assessment, and synthesis planning through shared context and typed scientific tools. The framework uses pretrained language models without task-specific parameter training; its contribution lies in the orchestration, evidence control, tool-mediated execution, and evaluation of the resulting workflow.

The revision substantially strengthens the manuscript. We replace broad superiority claims with task-aligned comparisons, explicitly separate direct-model and FROGENT-assisted conditions, report model and tool boundaries, add molecular novelty and pocket-compatibility analyses, and provide licensed MDockPeP2 reference-redocking evidence. We also distinguish known-candidate recovery from de novo discovery and position FROGENT relative to Prompt-to-Pill, CLADD, and Robin. These changes clarify both the scientific advance and its present limitations.

We believe the manuscript is relevant to *Communications Biology* because it addresses a practical methodological problem at the intersection of computational biology, drug discovery, and scientific AI: how heterogeneous models and evidence sources can be organized into a transparent, testable decision process. The accompanying repository and revision package provide the code, evaluation assets, and documented execution boundaries needed to inspect the work.

Thank you for considering the revised manuscript.

Sincerely,

Junkai Ji, corresponding author

on behalf of all authors
